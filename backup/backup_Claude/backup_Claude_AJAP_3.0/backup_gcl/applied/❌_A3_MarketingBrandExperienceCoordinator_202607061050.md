## 3. Application Tailoring
• Lead with bilingual and cross-cultural fluency as the primary differentiator — the Rednote requirement and Mandarin preference are rare in the Australian market; anchor the opening to authentic community engagement capability rather than generic marketing experience
• Reference measurable KOL and campaign outcomes from KE client work — focus on campaign types, channels managed, and documented performance metrics without naming restricted client names beyond those already in the resume
• Highlight the data-driven discipline behind creative output — cite specific metrics (e.g. 366% traffic, 25% YoY revenue, 12x SEM) to position Culous above typical content coordinators who cannot demonstrate analytical accountability
• Pre-empt the seniority gap in exactly one sentence framed as a deliberate Australian market entry, anchored to a concrete achievement to convey that the step is strategic rather than reactive
• Keep tone coordinator-appropriate throughout — emphasise readiness to produce, execute, and collaborate; avoid executive or transformation language that reinforces the seniority gap rather than neutralising it
