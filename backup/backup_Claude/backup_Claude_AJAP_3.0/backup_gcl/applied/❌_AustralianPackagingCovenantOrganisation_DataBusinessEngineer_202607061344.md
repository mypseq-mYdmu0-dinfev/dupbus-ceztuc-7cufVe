## 1. Employer
APCO (Australian Packaging Covenant Organisation) is a not-for-profit co-regulator administering Australia's national packaging covenant under NEPM 2011, with approximately 40 staff across Sydney and Melbourne. It sits at a strategic inflection point as voluntary self-regulation gives way to mandatory enforcement from 2026, driving urgent and expanding demand for robust data infrastructure, governance capability, and compliance reporting systems. Its national environmental accountability mandate grants it outsized influence relative to its compact headcount.
