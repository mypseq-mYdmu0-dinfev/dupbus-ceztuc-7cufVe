## 1. Employer
Opticare Pty Ltd is an Australian-owned B2B optical industry supplier founded in 1984, headquartered in Auburn, NSW, with offices in Perth and Brisbane. Serving independent optometry practices via wholesale lens manufacturing, frames, diagnostic instruments, and the proprietary OptiEdge cloud-based practice management platform, the business employs approximately 35 people with estimated revenue of AUD $7–8M. Recent dual lens launches (Optilux and Essential, built on German technology) and active multi-state hiring signal a deliberate growth phase rather than mere market maintenance.
