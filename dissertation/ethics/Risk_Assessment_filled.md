**Main hazards**

| Relevant hazard | Key issues and how the risk is currently controlled | Additional measures to reduce the risk | Final risk rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electrical equipment, data loss | All data is backed up regularly on a password-protected drive and on UoL OneDrive. The drive is formatted, and the OneDrive data deleted, once the project is complete. | None required. | Low |
| Late or lone working | Interviews are scheduled in accordance with the participants’ preferences. The Participant Information Sheet and consent form communicate the interview length, and effective communication is maintained throughout. Interviews are conducted online only, which removes physical lone-working exposure. | None required. | Low |
| Privacy of the interview setting | Online interviews are conducted one-to-one via Microsoft Teams, which is supported by the University of Liverpool, linked to the student researcher’s UoL account, and protected by a password. Participants are asked to join from a private space of their choosing. | None required. | Low |
| Psychological sensitivity of the topic (participant discomfort) | Questions are held at the professional-observation level rather than personal or emotional disclosure, and none address personal distress. Participation is voluntary, and participants may skip any question, pause, or withdraw at any time. The Participant Information Sheet signposts sources of support, including a trusted colleague, a general practitioner, or a confidential support service. | Interview guides are reviewed against the safe-framing test before use. | Low |
| Researcher wellbeing (hearing difficult accounts) | The professional framing of the questions limits exposure to distressing accounts. The researcher keeps a reflexive record and can space the interviews to allow recovery time. | Supervisor available for debriefing if needed. | Low |
| Data security and confidentiality | All interviews are held through the UoL Teams account, and recordings and anonymised transcripts are stored on UoL OneDrive. Identifiers are removed, the original audio is deleted after transcription, and no employer-identifying details are solicited. | None required. | Low |

L = low; M = medium; H = high

**Emergency arrangements**

| Emergency situation | Additional control required |
|---|---|
| A participant becomes distressed during the interview | Pause or stop the interview, remind the participant of their right to withdraw, and signpost appropriate sources of support |
| Data breach or loss | Report to the supervisor and the University, change the relevant credentials, and follow UoL data-protection procedures |
