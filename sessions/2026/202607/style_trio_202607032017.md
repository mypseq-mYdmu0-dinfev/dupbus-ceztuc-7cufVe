# Style Trio, Round 2 (4 Styles, Same 3 NEW Scenarios)

*Applying round 1’s feedback, e.g. no bare-name openers, elegant economy (not curtness) for `#style-uc`, `believe` over weak cognition verbs, no indirect meeting-invites, and a lighter touch in `#style-vic` on archaic words that still feel modern-weak.*

## Scenarios
- **A.** Declining to write a recommendation for a former junior colleague, Tom, whose work isn’t yet strong enough for the role (asked by Harriet)
- **B.** Holding a quoted price firm against a client, Oliver, pushing for a further discount
- **C.** Owning a scheduling error that inconvenienced a colleague, Priya

---

## `#style-cy`

- **A.** Hello Harriet, I’ve thought about this properly rather than giving you a quick answer, and I don’t believe I’m the right person to put my name to Tom’s application, much as I’d like to help him along. My experience of his work doesn’t yet stretch far enough to speak to what this particular role needs, and I’d rather tell you that plainly than write a thin reference that does him no favours either. Happy for him to use me as a reference for anything closer to what I’ve actually seen him do.
- **B.** Hello Oliver, I’ve had a proper look at whether there’s more room to move, and honestly, there isn’t, not without taking on work at a loss. I’d rather be straightforward with you about that now than agree to a rate I’d quietly resent later. If the budget genuinely can’t stretch to this, I’d understand completely, and would still love to find a smaller way to work together if one exists.
- **C.** Hello Priya, this one’s on me. I double-booked us against the Thursday review without checking properly first, and I know that’s landed you with a scramble that wasn’t yours to have. I’ve already moved my side to clear it, and I’ll build in a proper buffer before I commit either of us to this kind of clash again.

## `#style-uc`

- **A.** Hello Harriet, I fear Tom’s experience does not yet extend to what this particular role requires. I’d rather say so plainly now than have my name attached to an application it cannot yet support.
- **B.** Hello Oliver, I’m afraid the figure I gave you was already the accommodating one. There is regrettably no further generosity left in the arithmetic.
- **C.** Hello Priya, that clash was entirely of my making, I’m afraid. I’ve already cleared it from my side, and I don’t expect it to happen again.

---

## `#style-wit`

- **A.** Hello Harriet, I’m afraid my name and Tom’s application haven’t quite been introduced yet.
- **B.** Hello Oliver, I admire your optimism far more than I can afford to reward it.
- **C.** Hello Priya, I’ve turned scheduling into a competitive sport, and I’m losing.

---

## `#style-vic`

- **A.** Hello Harriet, it is with genuine reluctance that I must decline to lend my name to Tom’s application, for I have not yet observed in his work the breadth this particular role would demand of him, and I would sooner disappoint you now than mislead you at a later and less forgiving hour.
- **B.** Hello Oliver, it is with undiminished goodwill that I find myself unable to move the figure further, for I have already extended what generosity the arithmetic permits, and I should be doing neither of us a kindness were I to promise more than the sum could later bear.
- **C.** Hello Priya, it is with unreserved embarrassment that I must own the fault for Thursday’s clash entirely mine, and I could not forgive the lapse in myself were I not to set it right before it costs you a moment more than it already has.
