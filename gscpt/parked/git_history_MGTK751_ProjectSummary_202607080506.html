## Research Topic: **Tall Poppy Syndrome (TPS) as a Management Challenge in Knowledge-Based Firms (KBFs)**

TPS = social phenomenon where high-performing individuals (Tall Poppies / TPs) are marginalised by peers in response to their standout performance. In KBFs (creating value via expertise/intellectual capital e.g. legal, accounting, consulting, engineering, IT, R&D), where individual excellence is the core product, TPS creates team cohesion erosion and KM governance risks as TPs logically withdraw from knowledge sharing when marginalised.

- **Research vehicle:** CYC (Culous Yu Consultancy) — simulated MCF (management consulting firm) specialising in KM (knowledge mgmt); boutique/specialist tier; Culous as founding chief consultant
- **Primary mgmt challenge (A):** team cohesion erosion + KM governance failure
- **Contingency/Discussion angles:** attrition cost (B), systemic cultural conditions (C)
- **Scope:** cross-firm KBF sampling (1 per org)
- **Method:** explanatory multi-case study (Yin, 2003; Zainal, 2007); semi-structured interviews as qualitative data collection technique; theoretically-informed (sensitising) thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke, 2006)
- **Primary participants (ppt):** TPs'TPs’ peers (TPPs) who experienced TPS dynamics; min. 6 pax
- **RQ1:** What conditions within KBFs exacerbate or alleviate TPS?
- **RQ2:** How do team members in KBFs experience TPS-related team dynamics?
- **Current phase:** A1R v2 — amended per Lu (Logs 16/18, approved); aim reframed to inform CYC'sCYC’s KM consulting practice; theoretical framework added; target DA/reviewer approval then ethics (timeline re-baselined; final A2/A3 23/11/2026)
- **Remarks:** British English + Hart'sHart’s quotation rule always
