With approximately 51-200 staff across six Australian offices (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Gold Coast, Canberra, Perth), MBM recently completed a brand refresh to reposition itself as a multi-disciplinary consultancy beyond its boutique cost-consultancy origins, and is listed as a supplier to the Australian Government with recognised ties to the property and construction sectors. Revenue stands at approximately A$20.8M, and 12 staff were promoted nationally in late 2025, signalling active internal growth.
