## 1. Employer
Cue Clothing Co. is an Australian fashion retailer established in 1968, operating two distinct brands from its Surry Hills (Sydney) studio. Cue targets contemporary professional women seeking quality workwear and occasion wear, while Veronika Maine serves a more refined and mature aesthetic. The business sustains loyalty in the mid-to-premium segment through local design, considered production volumes, and premium fabrics, differentiating on craftsmanship and brand trust rather than fast-fashion volume.
