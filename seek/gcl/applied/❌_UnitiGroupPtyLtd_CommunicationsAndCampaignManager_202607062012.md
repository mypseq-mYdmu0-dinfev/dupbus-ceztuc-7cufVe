## 1. Employer
Uniti Group is an Australian private digital infrastructure company taken private in July 2022 via a A$3.62 billion acquisition by a consortium of Brookfield Infrastructure Partners, HRL Morrison & Co., and Commonwealth Superannuation Corporation. It owns Opticomm, Australia's largest independent FTTP wholesale network operator, with a contracted/connected pipeline exceeding 565,000 premises across greenfield and brownfield developments nationwide. Having exited retail NBN reselling in 2023, it now operates wholesale fibre infrastructure alongside retail consumer brands on its own fibre footprint; a growth-oriented new CEO was appointed in 2025, signalling an active post-privatisation expansion phase.
