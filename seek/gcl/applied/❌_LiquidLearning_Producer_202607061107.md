## 1. Employer
Liquid Learning (founded 2004) is an Australian conference and professional-development business producing events across 30+ industry sectors spanning government, corporate, and not-for-profit; it serves thousands of professionals annually across Australia and New Zealand and holds NSW Government supplier status (buy.nsw panel). Its stated mission is to change the world by inspiring the leaders of today and creating the leaders of tomorrow. Glassdoor sentiment is mixed (2.9/5; 48% recommend): positives centre on structured training and a social culture, while recurring concerns cite high-pressure KPI targets, elevated staff turnover, and a noted gap between the work-life-balance themes promoted at its own conferences and internal workplace practice.
