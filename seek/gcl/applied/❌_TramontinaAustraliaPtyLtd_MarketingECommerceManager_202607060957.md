## 1. Employer
Tramontina Australia Pty Ltd is the Australian subsidiary of Tramontina, a Brazilian family-owned homeware and cookware manufacturer founded in 1911 (110⁺ years of heritage), distributing 22,000⁺ products across 120⁺ countries with roughly 10,000 employees globally. The local entity operates as a lean sales and distribution office in Seven Hills, NSW, making this Marketing & E-commerce Manager role exceptionally high in ownership and direct visibility within a small but well-backed team. The brand occupies a mid-to-premium positioning bridging professional craftsmanship with everyday accessibility, supported by the parent's global sustainability programme 'Tramontina Transforma', launched 2020.
